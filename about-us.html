About Hyphenet Enterprises

Headquartered in the premier commercial tech center of Nehru Place, New Delhi, Hyphenet Enterprises is India's trusted technology infrastructure partner delivering scalable IT rentals, cloud-ready servers, and computer-based examination infrastructure to 100+ corporations nationwide.

Who We Are

Enabling Digital Transformation Without Capital Bottlenecks

Hyphenet Enterprises was founded in 2019 with a singular mission: to eliminate the massive capital expenditures associated with enterprise IT procurement. In a business ecosystem where technology upgrades occur every 18 to 24 months, traditional hardware ownership strains working capital, slows down workforce expansion, and introduces depreciation liabilities.

Operating from our corporate headquarters at 713, Devika Tower 6, Nehru Place, South Delhi, Hyphenet has established regional logistics, staging, and technical support nodes across Gurugram, Noida, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad. We maintain an active inventory buffer of over 5,000 commercial-grade computing systems, high-density multi-socket servers, Cisco routing switches, and high-resolution audit surveillance gear.
